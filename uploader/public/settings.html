Reader comments

Off (default): the comment form is hidden on every story and POST /comments returns 409. Approved comments already visible stay visible. Per-post "Allow comments" has no effect while this is off. Turning it off does not delete any comments — it is the reversible rollback for the whole comments feature. Every new comment is held for moderation. Saving this does not rebuild the site.
